Tips for Real estate agents

  • Enter commissions after your brokerage split if your 1099 shows only your share. If the 1099 shows the gross commission, put the split in brokerage fees instead.
  • Client gifts are deductible up to $25 per person per year.
  • Driving to showings and listing appointments counts as business miles.

Questions

What percentage of my commission should I save for taxes?

It depends on your profit and filing status, which is why the calculator shows your own set-aside rate. For many agents it lands somewhere around 20–30% before any state tax.

My income is lumpy. Do I still pay four equal amounts?

Equal payments are simplest. If most of your closings come late in the year, the IRS annualized method (Form 2210, Schedule AI) may lower early-quarter penalties.

Is this calculator accurate?

It follows IRS brackets, standard deductions and the Social Security wage base for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35), and it simplifies a few things: no credits, no itemized deductions, no additional 0.9% Medicare tax, and a simplified QBI deduction. Treat it as a planning estimate, not tax advice.
