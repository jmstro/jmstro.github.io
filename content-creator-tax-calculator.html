Tips for Content creators

  • Free products from brands are income at fair market value when you receive them as payment.
  • Gear: equipment used for content can usually be deducted in full the year you buy it.
  • Contractors: if you pay an editor $600 or more, you may need to send them a 1099-NEC (rising to $2,000 for payments made after 2025).

Questions

Do I owe taxes on YouTube money if I made under $600?

Yes. All income is taxable, and self-employment tax applies once your net earnings reach $400.

Is my phone deductible?

The business-use share is. If you use your phone 60% for content, deduct 60% of the cost and the plan.

Is this calculator accurate?

It follows IRS brackets, standard deductions and the Social Security wage base for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35), and it simplifies a few things: no credits, no itemized deductions, no additional 0.9% Medicare tax, and a simplified QBI deduction. Treat it as a planning estimate, not tax advice.
