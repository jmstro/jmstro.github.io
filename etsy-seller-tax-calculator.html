Tips for Etsy, Shopify & eBay sellers

  • Start from gross, then subtract fees: your 1099-K reports gross receipts. Etsy, PayPal and Shopify fees are deductible, so include them.
  • Cost of goods: materials only count when you sell the item (Schedule C Part III). Unsold inventory stays on the books.
  • Sales tax you collected through the marketplace isn't your income. Leave it out if your report includes it.

Questions

Is selling on Etsy a hobby or a business?

If you sell regularly to make a profit, the IRS treats it as a business: you report it on Schedule C, can deduct expenses, and owe self-employment tax on the profit.

I sold old personal stuff on eBay. Is that taxable?

Selling personal items for less than you paid is generally not taxable profit. This calculator is for items you make or buy to resell.

Is this calculator accurate?

It follows IRS brackets, standard deductions and the Social Security wage base for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35), and it simplifies a few things: no credits, no itemized deductions, no additional 0.9% Medicare tax, and a simplified QBI deduction. Treat it as a planning estimate, not tax advice.
