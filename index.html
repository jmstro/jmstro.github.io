Tips for Freelancer & 1099

  • Home office: the simplified method allows $5 per square foot of a space used regularly and only for work, up to 300 sq ft ($1,500).
  • Health insurance: if you pay for your own and aren't eligible for an employer plan, the premiums are usually deductible on Schedule 1 (not entered here).
  • Retirement: a SEP-IRA or Solo 401(k) contribution lowers income tax, but not self-employment tax.

Questions

Do I have to pay quarterly if I also have a W-2 job?

Not always. If your paycheck withholding covers enough of your total tax (see the safe-harbor result above), you can skip quarterly payments. Enter your W-2 wages and withholding to check.

What happens if I miss a quarterly payment?

The IRS charges an underpayment penalty, which works like interest on the amount you were short for each quarter. Paying late is better than not paying, and you can catch up by the January due date to limit it.

Is this calculator accurate?

It follows IRS brackets, standard deductions and the Social Security wage base for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35), and it simplifies a few things: no credits, no itemized deductions, no additional 0.9% Medicare tax, and a simplified QBI deduction. Treat it as a planning estimate, not tax advice.
