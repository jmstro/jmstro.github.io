Tips for House cleaners

  • Miles between clients count, and so do trips to buy supplies.
  • Helpers: payments to subcontractors are deductible. Send a 1099-NEC to anyone you paid $600 or more ($2,000 for payments made after 2025).
  • Uniforms with your logo, gloves and other protective gear are deductible.

Questions

Do I need to report cash payments?

Yes. All payments for your cleaning work are taxable income, whether cash, Venmo, Zelle or check.

Do I need an LLC to deduct expenses?

No. A sole proprietor reports income and expenses on Schedule C. An LLC doesn't change your federal income or self-employment tax on its own.

Is this calculator accurate?

It follows IRS brackets, standard deductions and the Social Security wage base for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and 2027 (Rev. Proc. 2026-35), and it simplifies a few things: no credits, no itemized deductions, no additional 0.9% Medicare tax, and a simplified QBI deduction. Treat it as a planning estimate, not tax advice.
